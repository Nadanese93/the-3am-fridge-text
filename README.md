## Entry

> 3am. You named the car, the plants, even the Wi-Fi. Six years of night shifts, and I am still just 'the fridge.'

`main.py` writes the entry byte-identical to `/output/entry.txt` (or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`).
